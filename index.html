Wordwyrm is a two-part word game from Poppy Studios. First, guide your wyrm to collect seven letters. Then use those letters to build as many words as you can.

The heart of the game

One round, two parts

You do not need to collect letters in spelling order. Gather any seven letter tiles during the slithering portion; the separate word-building portion begins afterward.

1 Slither and collect

Swipe to steer the wyrm around the board. Collect seven letter tiles while avoiding collisions.

2 Build and score

Use your collected letters to make words before time runs out. Longer and higher-value words score more.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I steer the wyrm?

Swipe up, down, left, or right anywhere on the gameplay area. New players receive an interactive steering tutorial during their first game.

Do the letters have to spell a word while I collect them?

No. The slithering portion is only for gathering seven letters. Afterward, Wordwyrm moves to a separate screen where you can rearrange those letters and build words in any order.

Which game mode should I choose?

Classic is the standard Wordwyrm experience. Relaxed removes the pressure of losing lives, while Hard increases the challenge for experienced players. You can change modes from the main menu.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open Wordwyrm, choose Remove Ads on the main menu, and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that originally purchased Remove Ads.

How do I change my advertising privacy choices?

Open Privacy Choices inside Wordwyrm whenever that control is available in your region. Wordwyrm requests contextual, non-personalized ads and does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission.

Where is my score and progress stored?

Your scores, settings, tutorials, and saved run are stored locally on your device. Wordwyrm does not require a player account.
